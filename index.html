Welcome to Trace-IQ Enterprise 🚀

Trace-IQ is your autonomous requirements intelligence platform. We bridge the gap between high-level stakeholder briefs and agile engineering execution. Follow this quick workflow guide to master your workspace.

📖 First-Time User Glossary

RTM (Requirements Traceability Matrix): A master document linking business goals directly to technical code components.
Atomic User Story: A standalone feature requirement written from the end-user's perspective.
BDD (Behavior-Driven Development): Translating requirements into clear, testable Gherkin scenarios.
Code Drift: Unintended discrepancies between what was planned in the PRD and what was actually pushed to GitHub.

Step-by-Step Workspace Workflow

1

🤖 AI Decomposition

Paste raw meeting transcripts or product notes here. Our AI engine extracts atomic user stories, estimates risk, and drafts component routes automatically.

Action: Go to AI Decomposition, paste notes, and click run.
2

📋 RTM Traceability Matrix

Your centralized command center. View all approved business requirements, track and edit their risk tiers (High/Med/Low), and manage components.

Action: Review synced requirements, change risks inline, or add new items.
3

📌 Sprint Kanban Board

Manage your team's live agile sprint workflow. Move requirements across To Do, In Progress, Review, and Done with real-time updates.

Action: Syncs in real-time across your entire enterprise team via Supabase.
4

📖 PRD Wiki Workspace

Collaborative product documentation modeled after Confluence. Keep your architecture notes, security guidelines, and specs centralized.

Action: Edit your living architecture documents and save instantly.
5

💬 RTM AI Copilot

Your intelligent assistant for sprint planning. Query your entire backlog in plain English to instantly surface high-risk dependencies.

Prompt Example: "Show me all high-risk security requirements."
6

🧪 BDD & Testing Suite

Automatically turn abstract user stories into structured Gherkin test scenarios (Given/When/Then) and integration test suites for QA.

Action: Select a requirement to generate acceptance criteria.
7

🛡️ Compliance Cross-Audit

Cross-reference your active sprint requirements against rigorous frameworks like **SOC2 Type II**, **GDPR**, and **HIPAA**.

Action: Select your regulatory framework to audit coverage.
8

📊 Analytics & Admin

Export your full RTM package as CSV or JSON, review audit trails, and manage company codes if you are an enterprise administrator.

Action: Monitor coverage metrics and export reports.
